Sensitivity-kernel acceleration
Timings, validation and remaining limits on Tunya
The approved acceleration campaign is complete. The latest native candidate reduces the degree-1536
unprofiled runtime from 536.442 to 313.361 seconds (41.6%), with byte-identical scientific outputs. The
previous accelerated candidate remains faster at degrees 128 and 384. Global synthesis/storage/threading
checks pass internally, but its frozen comparison against the original synthesis route still fails. No
sensitivity-kernel figures or requested high-degree complete 179-side global volume were generated.

1. Fixed experiment and comparison domains

The observable is windowed vertical-displacement energy,

The PREM500 model uses a source at 10◦ N, 20◦ E, depth 50 km, and receiver at 45◦ N, 75◦ E, depth 3
km. The nominal band is 0–10 mHz, flat to 8 mHz and tapered to 10 mHz. Sampling is 5 s, record
duration 3600 s, FFT length 1024 and period 5120 s; damping is 5/3600 s−1 . There are 52 physical bins

remain distinct. Contacts are off; source-shell contact contributions are omitted.
At high degree the native DSpec/YSpec comparison uses the same 64-angle, 179-side diagnostic,
not a complete global volume. Global diagnostics use the complete product-bandwidth grid, with
nθ = 2L + 1 and nϕ = FastFFTSize(4L + 1), on eight sides at low degree, two at L = 128, 384, and one
fluid side at L = 1536. Global timings must not be compared to YSpec’s 64-angle timing as if their
output domains matched.1

5. Validation, unresolved discrepancies and resolution

All 17 final-native jobs and 11 native comparison reports pass; all 19 global jobs and nine internal exact
comparisons pass; the post-analysis queue’s four additional paired exact checks pass. Native and global
scientific-array comparisons are byte-exact where reported, work counters are unchanged by the final
allocation fix, recorded inputs remain unchanged and process cleanup is verified. At degree 1536 the
energy is 8.5734206967 × 10−10 m2 s and the q · p versus 2E relative residual is 6.03 × 10−16 .
Global scientific promotion remains held. The original-direct versus global comparison at degrees
3 and 12 exceeds the frozen 2 × 10−13 threshold in the L and fluid-bulk channels (roughly 3.1–4.3 × 10−13
scaled differences). Internal threading/storage parity does not clear this separate gate, and no tolerance
was relaxed. These differences are distinct from the inherited DSpec/YSpec discrepancies: at 1536 the
saved solid-channel scaled differences include about 42.8% in C, 34.1% in F and 15.6% in L on the
diagnostic points. The fixed-degree waveform agreement is much better (relative L2 ≈ 4.64 × 10−5 ,
energy difference ≈ 2.68 × 10−5 ). Neither proves local-kernel agreement.2
The bounded resolution comparison uses a 720-point source-to-receiver great-circle arc, a 5-degree
angular exclusion at each endpoint, and approximate bilinear angular interpolation on two native sides.
There is no radial interpolation. Between degrees 128 and 384, max-scaled differences are 8.22% for the
selected fluid bulk trace and 8.29–17.65% across active solid channels; norm-scaled differences are 8.66%
and 8.93–16.63%. This does not establish convergence. The earlier degree-384 to 1536 energy difference
is about 2.93% relative to the degree-1536 value (2.84% relative to degree 384), so those degrees must
remain labelled exploratory.

6. Decision and next approved boundary

Use the validated native route for initial exploratory sections and depth slices, retaining raw values,
one-sided interfaces, contact omissions and explicit scales. At low degree v4 is currently faster; v5 has
the demonstrated large-degree gain. The global route remains experimental until its original-route
numerical gate is resolved. Its complete 179-side, degree-1536 output would occupy approximately
151.47 GiB; that requested high-degree full volume has not been materialised in this phase.
The proposed figure configuration keeps the same source, receiver, window and band, with kernel-
bandwidth-derived section sampling (4L + 1 points around the full great circle) and selected native
depths near 96.446, 600 and 1019.368 km. Degree 384/1536 panels must be exploratory. Receiver lifts,
Sobolev/Riesz gradients and receiver-point peak convergence remain deferred, as requested; they are
not prerequisites for the initial figures.
After figures, the largest remaining native targets are genuine batched SEM recovery, angular synthesis
and numerical LU. A vertical-receiver adjoint specialisation is a plausible further reduction, but has not
been implemented or validated here. The low-degree assembly/scaling/LU regression needs a bounded
compiler/memory investigation if further acceleration is requested. No additional optimisation campaign
or figure generation starts automatically.
