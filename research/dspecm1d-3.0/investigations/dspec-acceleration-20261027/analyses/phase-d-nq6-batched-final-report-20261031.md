# Phase D: NQ6 mesh selection and batched angular reconstruction

## Outcome

The isolated Phase D candidate met the requested 0.1% solid-field and full 35-bin lnVs-kernel comparison against the frozen NQ10 C2 reference at NQ6, mesh scale 0.9, `h=0.02035382429229197` (`relative_error` input remains `1e-5`). The user-selectable `DSPEC_PHASE_D_REQUESTED_RELATIVE_ACCURACY` controls selection/checking; only the requested `1e-3` setting is certified here. The full field comparison covers 45,122 common one-sided points across 35 bins. It used 208 native sides and all 35 frequency bins. The nominal NQ6 mesh (`h=0.022615360324768855`) missed the requested target and is preserved; the first approved refinement passed, so no 0.8 mesh was run.

On the selected mesh, the largest solid complex forward/adjoint field relative-L2 error was `2.19525e-4` (forward, bin 39, source population, section 1). The largest solid complete 35-bin summed kernel error was `1.45092e-4` (material-interface population, section 0). Waveform maximum was `1.19144e-6`; measurement-rate-norm error was `2.56198e-7`. Fluid subgroup results remain diagnostics and are not included in the solid acceptance claim. Common-point values use direct candidate SEM polynomials and derivatives at the same one-sided physical sample locations; there is no kernel interpolation or smoothing.

The same-grid native comparison ran the same build-v2 executable with only the scalar-only control changed. Driver elapsed was `9.32223s` scalar and `7.76639s` batched: batching reduced this one matched run by `16.69%` (1.200x). Supervisor elapsed was `9.71016s` and `8.10290s`. Output traces, spectra and components matched exactly; raw along/across arrays differed by at most `2.23e-15` relative-L2. The batched profile driver elapsed was `7.72444s`; its master pipeline wall scope was `7.72404s`. The selected-mesh gravity error was `2.6645e-15` absolute nondimensional and its time-shift identity was `0.9999999999999994`.

The earlier NQ10 C2 timed run took `15.89061s` driver time and `16.29612s` supervisor time, with 293 native sides. Phase D’s `7.76639s` run is about 51.1% lower in driver elapsed, but it is a different validated mesh/order and had 208 sides. These two counts are stated as 293 versus 208. Treat this as contextual end-to-end evidence, not an isolated same-work speedup.

Exactly one matched YSpec refresh completed at the same requested NQ6 geometry and frequency grid. Its driver time was `4.29867s` (`4.52717s` supervisor), versus `7.76639s` for DSpec batched. The YSpec command used CLI workers 8, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`, `OMP_THREAD_LIMIT=8`, and explicitly unset `DSPEC_FREQUENCY_WORKERS`. Its result JSON’s `frequency_workers=1/solve_workers=8` is fallback metadata and does not independently prove the internal scheduling. The requested node, angle, mesh and frequency-grid files were byte-identical across all four timing jobs. On elapsed time DSpec took 1.81x YSpec, but the raw arrays are not interchangeable and the cross-backend difference is not an accuracy gate. YSpec-versus-DSpec output differences are diagnostic only: waveform/component relative-L2 values were about `1.4e-5` to `4.2e-5`, while raw along/across arrays differed by `12.27%` and `3.68%`. The 0.1% acceptance budget was tested against the frozen NQ10 SEM reference, not YSpec; this refresh is timing context, not a YSpec accuracy or discrepancy study.

## Validation and resources

The selected-mesh checker passed scalar-versus-batched and common-versus-tensor controls at about `1e-15`, the full 35-bin accuracy comparison, exact-zero support, gravity and time-shift controls. The separate L3 batched finite-difference control passed with relative error `6.44026e-9` against the inherited `2e-4` limit; frozen baseline trace, spectrum and components matched exactly. L3 gravity error was `1.9984e-15` and time-shift identity `0.9999999999999994`.

The selected-grid profile recorded 35 forward-spectrum calls, 32 unit-adjoint calls and 3 exact unit-adjoint skips for bins 7, 8 and 41; forward work remained on all 35 bins. Tiled angular reconstruction scope calls were 32. Its `21.9173s` worker elapsed sum overlaps across workers and is not wall time and must not be subtracted from driver elapsed. Other large worker-sum scopes were modal workspace capture (`24.6697s`) and projected radial modes to strain tables (`4.6087s`). For each worker these scopes are consecutive and their elapsed sums partition worker time. Across concurrent workers their sums overlap in wall time; they are not shares of master wall and cannot be converted directly into time saved. Master-side measurement, reduction and output scopes were small in this run. The derived per-worker angular temporary peak bound was 17,940,200 bytes; observed batched-native RSS was 1.1194 GiB, scalar RSS 1.1591 GiB, profile RSS 1.1139 GiB, and YSpec RSS 0.3287 GiB. The supervisor observed 9 threads including itself; DSpec used 8 frequency workers with inner numerical libraries limited to 1. Build cap was 8 threads/32 GiB; numerical jobs were capped at 20 threads/16 GiB with the campaign aggregate ceiling 50. The accepted `USER_SPACE_SESSION_WATCHDOG` is user-space resource supervision, not kernel aggregate accounting.

All work remains in an isolated candidate. No production integration or discrepancy investigation was performed. The nominal-mesh miss and prior prelaunch staging failure remain preserved with their receipts.
