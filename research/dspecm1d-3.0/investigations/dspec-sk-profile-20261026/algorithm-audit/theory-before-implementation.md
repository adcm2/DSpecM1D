# DSpecM1D SK theoretical audit, 26 October 2026

Read-only audit of the accepted Phase 1 source snapshot. Windowed3DFields.h and Windowed3DKernels.h hashes were checked live and match final_provenance.json. No compilation, scientific convergence sweep, or production edit was performed.

## Ranking and prototype verdict

1. **Persistent tensor-grid sampling plan:** strongest immediate priority. Geometry, element-side identity, interpolation maps, Wigner/Legendre tables, phases, relative spin rotations, and angular-to-point addressing are independent of frequency. Build once per physical grid/frame/mesh signature, with semantic angular IDs rather than a vector containing whichever theta subset happened to fall into a 4096-point chunk. A 56-trace by 1352-angle tensor grid should reuse the same angular plan for every trace and frequency. Parent's measured 1989 Wigner builds and 1971 coefficient builds at only three bins confirm that this is a real cost, not hypothetical. Keep cache accounting and output tiling bounded; mesh changes must invalidate geometry plans.

2. **Share one factorization and solve only minimal receiver bases:** exact and worthwhile. Current solveFrequencyModes factors each H once for forward and again for adjoint. Its global weighted adjoint has 2l+1 RHS columns, plus a three-column supplemental receiver basis. For the present common receiver_depth contract, SEMReceivers.h proves receiver radial rows N=E: spheroidal rank at most two, toroidal at most one, radial at most one. All receivers share these radial bases. Use H=K_l-z^2 M_l, X=H^{-1}B, V=H^{-H}Q, then reconstruct any weighted adjoint as V C. Select U/V receiver rows for spheroidal, one nonzero tangential row for toroidal, and the radial row for l=0. The global 2l+1 weighted adjoint and three-column basis are redundant if derived from these factors. Keep current public/global modal and contact consumers correct; a fast workspace may derive them lazily or reconstruct them exactly. Existing compact source widths 4 spheroidal, 2 toroidal, 1 radial already avoid full-m solves; further source rank reduction requires actual force/lift dependence checks.

3. **Contiguous synthesis, scalar SIMD versus matrix contractions:** benchmark both. Existing YSpec layout degree innermost and separate real/imag dot sums is an appropriate direct baseline. Current source pole uses at most five orders, adjoint pole at most three. At L=12, total angular columns qF=1+3+11*5=59, qA=1+12*3=37 per receiver. On tensor grids, synthesize with H_s(Pangle,q) C_s(q,Nradial*NfrequencyBlock), tiled so intermediates never retain the complete volume-frequency history. Compare scalar/vector dots, Eigen products, and one-thread OpenBLAS; small inner dimensions can make library dispatch/packing cost dominate. Group by radial trace before invoking products. Nongrid arbitrary points do not share a single coefficient matrix merely because they share an element.

4. **Fuse strain-only synthesis, accumulation, and relative spin rotation:** benchmark with and without the new persistent plan. KernelField3DSample currently still materializes full Field3DSample first and then copies. Keep only six forward and six adjoint strains where material kernels are requested; receiver displacement is a separate small output. Precompute frequency-independent angular factors and avoid maps, allocation, square roots, exponentials, and all unnecessary displacement/r-scaled-strain work inside dot loops. Fusion must happen after degree/order sums, so cross-degree and cross-family terms remain intact. The measured rotation cost is small relative to Wigner and degree synthesis, so this is a secondary optimization rather than the main explanation.

5. **Native-node derivatives plus exact r-strain interpolation bridge:** implement a bounded variant. For element degree p=NN-1, D_e=D_reference/J_e, J_e=EW(e)/2. Gather element-side nodal values including source lift only on its designated element, then evaluate derivatives by D_e times all retained columns. Native values require a gather, not general Lagrange evaluation. Let lambda=l(l+1), zeta=sqrt(lambda), eta=sqrt((l-1)(l+2)). The six r_model-scaled modal strain numerators are

   r U'; 2U-lambda V; -zeta/2 [rV'-V+U+i(rW'-W)]; +zeta/2 [rV'-V+U-i(rW'-W)]; zeta eta(V+iW); zeta eta(V-iW).

   Every numerator is a polynomial of degree at most p. Therefore precomputing these at GLL nodes and interpolating their values at arbitrary radii is algebraically exact. Divide by r_model only at r>0 to recover raw strains; apply existing SI scale conventions. Interpolating *raw* nodal strain is generally wrong because of 1/r. Native precomputation pays when enough sample radii lie in an element; direct cached arbitrary-radius interpolation can win for sparse sampling. Parent profile shows radial coefficients are already only about .0062 s, so expect modest end-to-end improvement on the current grid. Duplicated node positions must remain distinct (element,radius) traces. Never average interface/source-side derivatives or source lifts.

6. **Exact frequency-first pair contractions and exact low-rank compression:** implement bounded prototypes, especially Nf48. Do not replace this with a product of frequency-summed fields. For each strain pair and fixed radial trace, with local coefficient vectors f_k in C^qF and a_k in C^qA,

   f_k(x)=hF(x)^T f_k; a_k(x)=hA(x)^T a_k;
   C=sum_k conj(a_k) f_k^T;
   sum_k conj(a_k(x))f_k(x)=conj(hA(x))^T C hF(x).

   This retains every l,l',m,m' cross term and only pairs fields from the same frequency. Include any frequency-dependent weights inside C. Without low-rank compression, direct local-pole synthesis costs O(F P(qF+qA)); pair formation plus evaluation costs O(F qF qA + P qF qA), per spin pair, receiver, and radius. Pair evaluation cannot beat direct arithmetic until F>qF qA/(qF+qA), about 22.7 at L12. The angle-count crossover is P>F qF qA/[F(qF+qA)-qF qA] when the denominator is positive. At F48 this arithmetic crossover is about 43 angles, so P1352 is a legitimate candidate. Constants, complex arithmetic, spin pairs, memory, and BLAS layout still decide the winner.

   For C=U Sigma V^H, evaluating rank-k factors costs O(P k(qF+qA)), with rank<=min(F,qF,qA). There is no guaranteed rank smaller than 37 at L12. Exact numerical-rank retention can help when F greatly exceeds coefficient rank. Approximate truncation requires measured kernel/objective error; no singular-value threshold alone certifies Born accuracy. These cross-covariances are generally neither Hermitian nor positive semidefinite. Do not use an eigendecomposition that assumes either property.

7. **Common-frame harmonic recoupling plus global FFT:** cannot be dismissed as theoretically dominated on the present large tensor grid; include a bounded actual prototype at least L3/6/12 and F12/48. Rotate the receiver coefficients into the source or global harmonic frame, then recouple frequency-summed covariance to scalar kernel harmonics. Each scalar modulus kernel has bandlimit 2L, so at L12 there are at most (2L+1)^2=625 coefficients. In the source frame, forward |m|<=2 implies kernel |M|<=L+2, leaving at most 515 scalar coefficients. Two different pole frames cannot both retain their compact m bounds after a common-frame recoupling. In contrast, evaluating compact-frame pair products uses qF*qA=2183 terms. Sparse Gaunt recoupling has a potentially worthwhile output reduction, although its coefficient formation and rotation costs can dominate at small F. Implementing this only as a standalone benchmark is sufficient before choosing a production route; a claim of domination without measurement would be premature.

   Define the convention-safe recoupling constants by

   G[J,M;a,b,s] = integral(conj(Y0_JM) conj(Ys_a) Ys_b)dOmega / integral(abs(Y0_JM)^2)dOmega.

   Then kernel coefficient k_JM=sum_{a,b,s} G[J,M;a,b,s] C_s[a,b], with the modulus-specific bilinear factors and real-part operation. Nonzero terms obey |l-l'|<=J<=l+l' and M=m-m'. Spin cancels because a and f carry the same spin in a common frame. A real-valued final kernel can be represented through its conjugate harmonic symmetry, but damped complex field coefficients themselves do not have the naive real-field symmetry. Do not import a standard normalized-Y Gaunt prefactor without checking GSHTrans Wigner normalization and phase convention. Quadrature-defined precomputed G is a viable bounded reference if the projection quadrature resolves the complete triple product.

   An FFT in longitude is justified only on an equally spaced longitude grid in the synthesis frame. The global 26x52 angular tensor grid can support it after rotating compact pole coefficients to global full-m coefficients. An FFT of the five source-pole modes alone is unlikely to pay, and receiver-pole longitudes of a global tensor grid are not equally spaced. Fields of degree L need at least 2L+1 longitude samples for reconstruction; scalar kernels of degree 2L need at least 4L+1. Recovering all kernel harmonic coefficients by the usual Gauss-Legendre transform requires at least 2L+1 latitude nodes. Integration of a scalar degree-2L kernel alone is cheaper: L+1 Gauss-Legendre nodes and 2L+1 equally spaced longitude nodes suffice. Reconstruction and integration are different tasks. Degree 24 here is exclusively the scalar angular product representation of fields whose solve degree remains at most 12; it does not authorize high-degree radial solves or high-degree solver repairs.

## Exact contraction and rotation contract

With h=horizontalTrace, r=rr, p/m=radialPlus/radialMinus, P/M=horizontalPlus/horizontalMinus, and S=P0*L0^3, the code computes

K_A=-Re(conj(hA)hF)/S;
K_C=-Re(conj(rA)rF)/S;
K_F=-Re(conj(rA)hF+conj(hA)rF)/S;
K_L=-2 Re(conj(pA)pF+conj(mA)mF)/S;
K_N=-Re((conj(PA)PF+conj(MA)MF)/2-conj(hA)hF)/S.

Fluid K_kappa=-Re(conj(rA+hA)(rF+hF))/S. Structural fluid and low-degree zeros must match accepted semantics.

If cF=aF-i*bF and cA=aA-i*bA are the current unit spin rotations to global coordinates, set t=cF*conj(cA). A same-spin contraction in the two local frames is t^s*conj(a_s)*f_s for spins s=0,0,+1,-1,+2,-2. This avoids rotating both fields. For multiple receivers, rotate each adjoint into the source frame and sum before the final contraction, or contract each receiver separately using linearity; verify the same accumulation convention. Source/receiver polar gauges and coincident/antipodal geometries need explicit tests.

## Measurement, source, and numerical traps

* The real time window is a full-frequency linear map P. Compute qhat=P*weights on the complete nonnegative FFT grid, preserving damping removal, SI dt and length scales, and DC/Nyquist eta weights. It is not a diagonal frequency multiplier. A measurement originally nonzero in a narrow frequency band can produce qhat outside that band. Batching measurements is valid after each exact P* action; different windows cannot silently share one qhat.
* Frequency sum of field products has k=k' only; degree/order product has every l,l',m,m'. Summing per-degree kernels loses cross-degree terms. Combining spheroidal/toroidal strain coefficients before contraction preserves cross-family terms.
* Raw forward displacement receives -i/z when forming the receiver spectrum; adjoint RHS receives its conjugate. qhat already carries measurement-map SI factors. Do not add an extra time/FFT/quadrature factor to kernels. Preserve source-contact terms and the element-local lift; volume kernels alone do not capture the contact contribution.
* Zero qhat at one frequency permits skipping that frequency's adjoint/kernel work, but preserving returned raw/processed receiver spectra still requires the forward calculation. Zero individual components/directions can reduce receiver bases. Generic damping destroys naive negative-m conjugate symmetry of the full complex field, and H is complex symmetric rather than Hermitian. Do not replace H^{-H} with H^{-1}.
* r^2 K is polynomial degree at most 2p within an element for these modulus kernels. An optional dense-arbitrary-radius variant can accumulate that polynomial and sample it later, but p+1 native kernel values do not determine a degree-2p product. At least 2p+1 appropriate values or coefficient products are required. Raw point strain remains unavailable at r=0; preserve the existing r-scaled weighted-volume convention there.
* No frequency-to-frequency numeric factor reuse is exact because z changes H. Symbolic sparsity/order reuse may help, but must be supported by the existing equilibrated solver and benchmarked. Alternative low-rank updates, iterative solves, generic approximate compression, or a new indefinite complex-symmetric factorizer are not necessary first-stage prototypes.

## Minimal evidence matrix

Use L3/6/12, F1/3/12/48, existing fixed physical/discretization inputs, native tensor traces and an arbitrary-radius side-preserving sample set. Report cold plan-build time, warm end-to-end time, factorization/substitution/synthesis split, arithmetic counts, peak memory, and resulting outputs. Keep thread count one, and coordinate at most two compiler jobs across the team. Tiled frequency-first prototypes must count coefficient-storage memory as well as angular caches. Compare every modulus including fluid kappa, contact terms, objective, and returned receiver spectra. Include source-element traces, shared interfaces, l0/l1 structural zeros, pole/antipode angles, and multiple receivers/directions/windows. Reassociation changes rounding; use agreed absolute and relative tolerances and inspect the largest discrepancies before describing a variant as exact in numerical evidence. None of these tests establishes physical convergence.
