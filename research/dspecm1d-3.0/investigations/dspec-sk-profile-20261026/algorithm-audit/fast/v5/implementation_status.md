- End-to-end selectable routes are `scalar`, `eigen`, `openblas`, complex-basis `tensor`, and real-degree `tensor-real`; all invoke solver-derived pole synthesis and the ordinary point-kernel/contact/objective path. `DSFAST_BATCH_RESPONSES=1` is a real opt-in multi-objective source/basis-response cache route; contact-on batch mode fails strict gates and does not automatically fall back. `DSFAST_RSTRAIN_BRIDGE=1` is an actual selectable nodal r*strain-polynomial bridge. `DSFAST_NATIVE_DERIVATIVE=physical` is an actual cached native-node derivative alternative; default native path remains Dref/J.
- The tensor routes require a complete angle-set × element/radius trace product with unique angle samples on each trace. They reject ragged/permuted-as-incomplete inputs rather than silently falling back; arbitrary clouds use scalar/Eigen/OpenBLAS packed routes. Tensor radial coefficient tiles are bounded at 16 columns. Receiver suffix displacement still uses the packed evaluator.
- `SamplingPlanCache` is owned by one driver run and shared through its objective/frequency loops. Exact transformed frame/angle, exact radius bits plus element identity, angular labels, and semantic basis keys avoid approximate coordinate reuse and preserve duplicated element-side traces. Its shared 32 MiB retained-payload estimate is not a hard process-memory cap; once an item does not fit, the sampler builds that plan/table locally for the current operation and continues without persistence. It does not reorder or sparsify point outputs.
- `BatchResponseCache` is owned by one driver run and shared across objectives; its key is the live SEM object address, family, degree, and exact bit patterns of complex frequency. Forward and unit-adjoint RHSs are compared by exact matrix-entry bit signatures on reuse; response matrices and signatures are retained under 2,048 entries / 512 MiB. The unit basis is exact receiver-row rank reduction and weighted RHS reconstruction must be <=1e-12. This is safe for the driver's immutable SEM/operator; the key does not fingerprint a mutable operator matrix, so callers must not reuse the cache after changing one. Capacity, rank, key/signature, or reconstruction failures currently throw; they do not automatically retry the direct weighted-adjoint path. Contact sensitivity remains why this option is opt-in and contacts-off in the validated batch smokes.
- Implemented helper-only probes in `fast_engine.hpp` are `relativeSpinProduct`, `pairCovariance/evaluatePair`, `samePattern`, `SharedSymmetricFactor`, and standalone rank-basis routines; relative-spin reduction, frequency covariance, sparse symbolic reuse, and that shared-factor wrapper are not wired into the candidate route. The candidate does use its separate exact receiver-basis/batch implementation. Solver backend selectors `band`, `natural`, and `condense` are wired diagnostically but parent frozen-field/contact gates failed them; only `equilibrated` remains the reference-compatible candidate selection. Fluid divergence fusion and spin structural zeros are wired in packed synthesis, but a fresh frozen-production fluid gate remains pending.
- The 32 MiB sampling-plan and 512 MiB response-cache limits are retained payload estimates, not hard process-memory caps; they omit allocator/map nodes, some key storage, temporaries, and returned copies. Packet/modal exports are diagnostic and should remain disabled during headline timings; packet files can be very large at high degree/frequency.
